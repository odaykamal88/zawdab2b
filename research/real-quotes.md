# Real Quotes: MENA Digital Asset Compliance

Verified, verbatim quotes from real people and official sources about digital asset compliance in the UAE/MENA region. Each quote has a confirmed, live source URL.

## Quote 1 — VARA CEO (Henson Orser) on Licensing and Compliance Expectations

**Exact Quote:**
> "We are pleased to welcome BitOasis to the MVP Programme phase. The VARA ecosystem aims to strike a balance between value creation, risk mitigation and enhanced investment opportunities with consumer protection at its core. BitOasis has demonstrated a strong commitment to operating with a firm bias for regulation throughout the licensing process. One of VARA's founding principles is creating an equal opportunity regime for responsible VASPs and being able to onboard credible home-grown companies, like BitOasis, in addition to leading global platforms, allowing us to bring the shared learnings of our licensees to build the foundation for our global future economy."

**Person:** Henson Orser  
**Title:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Organization:** VARA (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority), Dubai  
**Context:** Statement issued on VARA's official website upon BitOasis becoming the first broker-dealer to secure an MVP Operational Licence — commenting on VARA's licensing standards and philosophy.  
**Source URL:** https://www.vara.ae/en/news/bitoasis-becomes-the-first-broker-dealer-to-secure-mvp-operational-licence-from-dubais-virtual-asset-regulatory-authority/  
**Date:** 1 May 2023

## Quote 2 — ADGM FSRA CEO on Regulatory Certainty for Digital Assets

**Exact Quote:**
> "The implementation of these changes marks a significant milestone in the evolution of the FSRA's framework for digital asset regulation. Through extensive consultation with industry stakeholders, we have further enhanced our framework to provide the regulatory certainty that industry participants need, while addressing the evolving risks of the digital asset ecosystem. We believe this further positions ADGM as a premier jurisdiction for digital asset-related activities and shows our commitment to fostering responsible innovation in financial services."

**Person:** Emmanuel Givanakis  
**Title:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Organization:** Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA), Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM)  
**Context:** Official statement issued on ADGM's website announcing the implementation of amendments to ADGM's regulatory framework for digital assets, including new virtual asset acceptance criteria, capital requirements, and product intervention powers.  
**Source URL:** https://www.adgm.com/media/announcements/adgm-fsra-implements-amendments-to-its-digital-asset-regulatory-framework  
**Date:** 10 June 2025

## Quote 3 — BitOasis CEO on Regulatory Commitment (VARA MVP Licence)

**Exact Quote:**
> "We are extremely proud to receive VARA's MVP Operational Licence. BitOasis was launched in Dubai in 2015 and the city has remained our home ever since. We remain committed to operating in accordance with applicable law, regulation and best practices from Dubai and across the region. ## Quote 5 — Rain CEO on the Value of Being Regulated

**Exact Quote:**
> "We're the first regulated cryptocurrency exchange in the Gulf and the greater Middle East. We really value regulation and its strong banking ties."

**Person:** Joseph Dallago  
**Title:** CEO and Co-Founder  
**Organization:** Rain Financial  
**Context:** Interview with 500 Global, discussing Rain's strategy after becoming the first company licensed by the Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB) as a crypto-asset service provider in 2019. Dallago linked the regulatory licence directly to Rain's ability to secure stable banking partnerships and serve customers across the GCC.  
**Source URL:** https://500.co/content/bahraini-crypto-exchange-rain-wants-to-become-a-global-player  
**Date:** March 2022

## Quote 6 — Dubai Ruler on the Purpose of Virtual Asset Regulation

**Exact Quote:**
> "The future belongs to whoever designs it. Today, through the virtual assets law, we seek to participate in the design of this new and rapidly growing global sector. Our step is a leap towards the future aimed at developing this sector and protecting all investors in it."

**Person:** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum  
**Title:** Vice President, Prime Minister of the UAE, and Ruler of Dubai  
**Organization:** Government of Dubai  
**Context:** Tweet published on 9 March 2022 by Sheikh Mohammed announcing Dubai's first law governing virtual assets (Law No. 4 of 2022) and the establishment of VARA. The statement was cited by multiple news outlets including Reuters and Blockworks covering the announcement.  
**Source URL:** https://blockworks.co/news/dubai-is-the-latest-government-to-roll-out-crypto-law-set-up-regulator  
**Date:** 9 March 2022
