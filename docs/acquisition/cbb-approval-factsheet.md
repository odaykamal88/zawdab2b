# CBB In-Principle Confirmation — Fact Sheet

> **CONFIDENTIAL — for diligence under NDA only.** Per caveat (iii) of the letter below, Zawda may not publicly announce CBB approval or the in-principle confirmation. This fact sheet, and the underlying letter, are shared confidentially with a prospective acquirer under NDA and must not be reproduced publicly, in a press release, or in publicly-shared marketing.

## The document
- **Type:** In-principle confirmation to proceed with the CBB licensing process (a first-stage acknowledgement — *not* a licence and *not* final approval).
- **Reference:** OG/156/2025
- **Date:** 3 June 2025
- **Issued by:** Central Bank of Bahrain, signed by **Khalid Humaidan, Governor**
- **Applicant:** Zawda W.L.L. (under formation), Kingdom of Bahrain
- **Application:** Crypto-Asset Service Provider / **Category 2** licence

## What it says (material terms, quoted)
- The CBB confirms the application "has been initially reviewed and received an *'in-principle' confirmation to proceed with the licensing process.*"
- It expressly **does not**:
  - (i) commit the CBB to final approval or granting a licence — the CBB "may decide to grant a different category of license or no license at all, at [its] discretion";
  - (ii) express any opinion on the fitness of proposed CEO/COO/GM or other controlled functions (assessed separately at the next stage);
  - (iii) **permit public announcements** that the applicant "has been finally approved by the CBB, whether an 'in-principle' confirmation has been provided… or otherwise." A violation "may result in [the] license application being cancelled."
- Applicants remain bound by Bahrain law (incl. the Commercial Companies Law); any inconsistency with the CBB Rulebook is construed in favour of the Rulebook.

## Scope of the Category 2 CASP (from the attached Schedule)
The applied-for licence is **custody-inclusive** and trading-capable. The Schedule (15 items, due within 6 months) requires, among others:
- Paid-up capital **BD 171,000** deposited (certified by a Bahrain-licensed retail bank).
- **Custody arrangement + private-key creation/management/controls + wallet-access management** per **Rule CRA-5.1.2 and Chapter 8 of the CRA Module**, seeking CBB approval on same (item 8).
- Demo of **KYC system, trading platform, and wallets management** (item 7).
- Liquidity-provider introduction for trading services (item 10); crypto-asset listing policy per CRA Module §4.3 (item 11).
- Board of ≥5 members (2 independent); Forms 3 for senior management/board; banking arrangements incl. client-money account; outsourcing agreements; per-activity risk assessments; auditor acceptance; independent third-party readiness reviewer; **professional-indemnity insurance BD 100,000** (CRA 4.8.2); draft MoA/AoA (CRA 1.2.9).
